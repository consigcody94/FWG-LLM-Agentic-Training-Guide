## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the FWG LLM Agentic Training Guide, pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- Age
- Body size
- Visible or invisible disability
- Ethnicity
- Sex characteristics
- Gender identity and expression
- Level of experience
- Education
- Socio-economic status
- Nationality
- Personal appearance
- Race
- Religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Veteran status

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Respectful Communication** | Using welcoming and inclusive language |
| **Constructive Feedback** | Providing and gracefully accepting constructive criticism |
| **Collaborative Focus** | Focusing on what is best for the community |
| **Empathy** | Showing empathy towards other community members |
| **Professionalism** | Maintaining professional standards in all interactions |
| **Accountability** | Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes |
| **Learning Orientation** | Being open to learning from others |

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Harassment** | Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks |
| **Discrimination** | Any form of discriminatory behavior or language |
| **Sexual Misconduct** | Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances |
| **Privacy Violations** | Publishing others' private information without permission |
| **Professional Misconduct** | Conduct unbecoming of federal professionals |
| **Security Violations** | Sharing classified or sensitive information |
| **Disruptive Behavior** | Sustained disruption of discussions or activities |

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported through the following channels:

1. **Direct Contact**
   - Email: conduct@federalworkinggroup.example.com
   - All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly

2. **Anonymous Reporting**
   - Use the anonymous reporting form (link provided internally)
   - Reports are treated confidentially

3. **GitHub Reporting**
   - Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for repository-specific issues

### Report Contents

When reporting, please include:
- Your contact information (optional for anonymous reports)
- Names of anyone involved
- Description of the incident
- Date, time, and location (if applicable)
- Any supporting documentation
- Any witnesses

### Response Process

```

│   Report    │────▶│   Review    │────▶│Investigation│────▶│  Decision   │
│  Received   │     │  (24 hrs)   │     │ (1-5 days)  │     │  & Action   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

### Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

#### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Minor unprofessional behavior

**Consequence**: Private, written warning with explanation of violation. May include request for public apology.

#### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: Single incident or series of minor violations

**Consequence**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with involved parties for specified period. Violations may result in ban.

#### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior

**Consequence**: Temporary ban from project interaction for specified period. No public or private contact with community members during ban.

#### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression

**Consequence**: Permanent ban from all project participation and community spaces.

## Federal Professional Standards

As a project supporting federal contracting, we hold ourselves to additional professional standards:

### Security Consciousness
- Never share classified or sensitive information
- Follow proper handling procedures for all data
- Report security concerns through appropriate channels

### Ethical Conduct
- Maintain integrity in all professional dealings
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Comply with federal ethics regulations

### Professional Development
- Support continuous learning for all participants
- Share knowledge appropriately
- Mentor and support newer community members

### Diversity and Inclusion
- Actively promote diverse perspectives
- Remove barriers to participation
- Value contributions from all backgrounds
